About

Who I am

I’m Yujie, a master’s student at BUPT’s State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology and a research intern on Kuaishou’s Foundation Model Team. I build multimodal models that understand change over time, from decade-long satellite image series to long-term memory in large language models.

I. Multimodal LLMs Long-term memory · Visual question answering → II. Temporal Vision Multi-temporal interpretation · Change captioning · Remote sensing →
