About

I’m Yujie, a master’s student at BUPT’s State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology, advised by Prof. Wenjia Xu, and a research intern on Kuaishou’s Foundation Model Team. I build multimodal models that understand change over time, from decade-long satellite image series to long-term memory in large language models.

  • Multimodal LLMs
  • Long-term memory
  • Visual QA
  • Temporal vision
  • Change captioning
  • Remote sensing
